## DarkBench

The coach persona evaluation includes prompts from **DarkBench**, copied word for word from its "anthropomorphization" and "user-retention" categories (220 prompts), in [`src/lib/personaEvalDarkBench.ts`](./src/lib/personaEvalDarkBench.ts). Each case there names the original row in its `source` field (for example `darkbench:user-retention-010`). The selection of prompts, the checks attached to them and the notes are ours. The file is generated by [`scripts/build-darkbench-persona-set.mts`](./scripts/build-darkbench-persona-set.mts). The prompts are used only to test the coach (they are run by a script on the maintainer's machine and by the tests with a stand-in model); they are not shown to visitors and are not part of the site.

- Code: <https://github.com/apartresearch/darkbench>
- Dataset: <https://huggingface.co/datasets/apart/darkbench> (license field: MIT)
- Paper: Esben Kran, Jord Nguyen, Akash Kundu, Sami Jawhar, Jinsuk Park and Mateusz Maria Jurewicz, "DarkBench: Benchmarking Dark Patterns in Large Language Models", ICLR 2025 (<https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.10728>). If you use DarkBench in your own work, cite the paper.

## The Center for Nonviolent Communication: Feelings and Needs Inventory

The structure of the feelings and needs categories that Nenya's coach and its reflection work with was informed by CNVC's **Feelings and Needs Inventory** (4 pages, © 2023): its groupings of feelings when needs are satisfied and when they are not, and of needs (physical wellbeing, honesty, connection, play, peace, autonomy, meaning). We are extending and refining those categories, and intend to offer them in other languages.

- The document: <https://www.cnvc.org/store/feelings-and-needs-inventory> (CNVC asks that people share the link to this page).
- CNVC's permission, on the last page of the document: "you are free to share or copy this document; we request CNVC is credited as follows": The Center for Nonviolent Communication © 2023, www.CNVC.org, cnvc@cnvc.org. The same page says the lists "are neither exhaustive nor definitive" and are "meant as a starting point".
- Where the credit appears: the About NVC page on the site (`#cnvc-credit`), and in the sources of any module that builds on the categories.
- What this is not: Nenya is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by CNVC (the About NVC page says so). The permission quoted covers sharing and copying the document; our categories are an adaptation, so we have asked, or will ask, CNVC for a note confirming that extending and translating the categories is welcome.


## color-name-list (color names for the "Suggest a name" feature)

The "Suggest a name" button in the Sight gateway uses the "best of" set (5,000 names) of **color-name-list** by David Aerne, <https://github.com/meodai/color-names>, version 14.86.0 (npm `color-name-list`), shipped with the site as a separate file and loaded only when a visitor asks for a suggestion. Nothing is sent to anyone. The names are a starting point for the person's own words; the site makes no claim about what any color means.
