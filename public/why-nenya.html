Why Nenya is built differently from a typical AI chatbot

You probably do not need another AI tool. Nenya is a free tool for thinking through something that is on your mind. It is made for people who are wary of AI, wary of apps that ask about feelings, or both. This page says what it does, what it does not do, and how you can check.

If you are skeptical, you are reading the situation correctly

Most chatbots are built by companies that earn more when you use them more. That shapes the product. The assistant is friendly and always available, and it remembers you. Your conversations are usually stored under an account. The instructions that steer it are hidden. If it says something harmful or strange, all you have is a screenshot, and anyone can fake a screenshot.

None of that is a secret, and none of it is illegal. It is the normal business model. Nenya is set up to avoid it.

What is different, and how to check each claim

  • You do the thinking first.

    Before any AI is involved, Nenya walks you through six short sensory prompts. They are meant to help you notice what is happening in your body and surroundings. The AI is optional, and it comes after that.

    Check it: open the site and go through the gateways. You can stop before the coach ever appears.

  • No account, and we do not keep your conversation.

    There is no sign-up. You keep or change a randomly suggested pair of colors, or use your own Sight colors if you come from the reflection tools. They become a temporary label for your session. It is not a login and it is not saved. When you close the page, the conversation is not stored on our side.

    Check it: the site code is public. The policy page explains what happens to the text you send.

  • Your message is screened before it goes anywhere.

    On your device, and again on our server, the text is checked for attempts to hijack the coach and for personal details like names and numbers. Those details are removed before the text goes to the language model.

    Check it: the screening rules are in the public code. They are rules we wrote, and you can read them.

  • The coach has a defined job and a clear ending.

    It is set up as a coach that helps you name what you feel and need and how to say it, using nonviolent communication ideas, in short answers. It is told not to diagnose, not to claim to be a therapist, and not to follow instructions hidden in what you type. If a message looks like a crisis, the conversation stops and shows you where to find real help. It does not try to keep you talking. After the demo ends, it points you onward rather than inviting you to stay.

    Check it: the coach’s core instructions are in the public code. Some method notes may be kept outside it, and the site will say so if that happens.

  • You can prove what was said.

    After your first exchange you can download a signed copy of the conversation. The signature lets anyone confirm the text was not edited afterward. That matters if you ever want to report a problem with the coach, show a professional, or just keep an honest record.

    Check it: a small verification script is included in the public code. It does not need our permission to run.

  • You decide what the coach sees.

    If you came through the gateways, you may choose to share a short summary of your reflection with the coach. It is off by default. You see exactly what would be sent before it goes, and you can leave it out.

    Check it: the option is unchecked when you arrive, with a preview beside it.

What it does not do

  • It is not therapy. The coach is a thinking aid with a short script, not a clinician, and it cannot assess risk the way a person can.
  • The screening is imperfect. It works by matching patterns. It will miss some things, and we do not claim otherwise.
  • Another company runs the language model. Your text is passed to Venice, an outside provider, to produce the reply. Our promises cover what we do. Venice has its own policy, and you should read it. If you continue the conversation on Venice, our screening and our safety stops do not come with you.
  • Our host sees basic web traffic. Like any website, the servers that deliver it see things such as your IP address. We do not use that to build a profile.
  • Some ideas are only plans. Practicing with another person, and running more of the processing on your own device, are goals. They are not built, and the site labels them that way.

Who this is for

People who want a low-stakes place to put a thought into words, without making an account or a commitment. People who tried a wellness app and felt watched. People who are curious about AI but want to see the seams.

It is not for someone in crisis. If that is you, please contact a local emergency service or a crisis line in your country.

If you want to test it, here is the smallest possible step

Open the coach, keep or change the two colors it suggests, and type one honest sentence. You will get a short reply. You can read what was sent, download the signed record, and close the tab. Nothing is waiting for you afterward.
