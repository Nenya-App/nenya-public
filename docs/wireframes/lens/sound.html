The Sound lens: four cards

All wireframes · Sight · Draft 2026-11-17 · plan: docs/lens-audit-sight-sound.md

How to read these

Controls on the left work with the keyboard; notes on the right give the tab order, what a screen reader says, what responds, and the copy draft. Emergency Exit is first in every tab order. These wireframes make no sound: where the real card plays, a line of text shows what would be heard.

Sound starts from Sight’s colors if they were added or chosen there, and offers its colors back to Sight. Both are offers, never requirements.

Choose the next note

What you would hear: the notes you have so far, in order.

Tab order

  1. Emergency Exit
  2. Put this down
  3. Start from my colors (only if colors exist), Surprise me
  4. Scale (one select), Hear this scale
  5. The six slots, then the seven note buttons
  6. Play, Undo, Clear, Next

A screen reader says

  • Each slot is named with its place and state: “Note 1 of 6, now: empty”. Choosing a note says its name and which slot it went into, politely.
  • The scale is one select with one “hear” button, not six buttons at once.

Responds live

  • Choosing a note plays it (only if the person has switched sound on); each slot shows its note.
  • Sound is off until asked. Without it, the card still works and the text line says what would be heard.

Tab order

  1. Emergency Exit
  2. Put this down
  3. Now or wish (radio group)
  4. Pitch, volume, speed (sliders)
  5. Sound, feel (radio groups)
  6. Play, Back to the original, Next

A screen reader says

  • Each slider: “Pitch, lower to higher, original.” Steps are five, with the original in the middle, named in words, never as numbers or notes.
  • The line “What you would hear” is the text twin of the sound and updates politely.

Responds live

  • Pitch is a transposition of the whole melody (about an octave each way, intervals kept, so it works for all six scales). Volume sets loudness. Speed sets the tempo. Sound changes the instrument. Feel changes how the notes are played (steady, flowing, light and separate, swaying).
  • Moving a control plays a short sample if sound is on; Play plays the whole melody. Try the controls: the line of text updates.
  • Now and wish are shaped separately, so the two states can sound different.

New in the player

Pitch (transposition) and the four feels are new in melodyPlayer.ts; speed, volume and timbre exist.

Sound · card 3 of 4 (only when available)

Make it longer? (optional)

You marked 2 palettes to be heard. Make a longer melody from them, one short phrase for each color.

What you would hear: one phrase for each of your 2 colors and 2 palettes, in order.

When it shows

Only when palettes were marked for sound in Sight (or colors exist for phrases). Otherwise this card does not exist and the lens goes from card 2 to the last card.

Tab order

  1. Emergency Exit
  2. Put this down
  3. Make it longer, Play it
  4. Skip, Next

A screen reader says

  • The count of palettes, then what each phrase is for. The longer melody reads as a short list of phrases with the same “what you would hear” text.

Notes

This is the existing long mode (longMelody), moved off the first card so that card stays simple.

Sound · card 4 of 4

Add Sound to your report?

This is what would be added:

  • Your melody: six notes, Western scale
  • Now: original pitch, medium volume, soft, steady
  • Wish: higher, softer, bell, flowing
  • A longer melody: none

Added. You can come back and change it.

If put down without adding, your report says: “Examined, not included: Sound.” Anything you made stays here for the visit, marked “not in your report yet”, and is never saved unless you add it. The sound file stays an option on the report page, made on your device.

Tab order

  1. Emergency Exit
  2. Put this down (top)
  3. The summary (a list)
  4. The free-text box
  5. Add this to my report, Put this down

A screen reader says

  • The summary is a real list. After Add, focus moves to “Added.” and it is read once.

States

Before, added (Update and Remove), examined only: the same three as Sight.

Notes

The free-text box is the old “describe it” step, now last and optional. The session colors form from colors added in Sight or Sound, never from anything merely tried.
