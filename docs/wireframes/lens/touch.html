Tab order

  1. Emergency Exit
  2. Put this down
  3. Texture, temperature, pressure, weight, sharpness (sliders)
  4. Back to the original surface, Skip, Next

A screen reader says

  • Each slider: “Temperature, frozen to burning, neutral.” The line “It feels: …” is the text twin and updates politely.

Responds live (try it)

  • The surface you picked is wrapped on an orb (the default shape; here a pattern stands in for the picture and a flat gradient for the light). Texture: how grainy it looks. Temperature: cool to warm tint. Pressure: the orb is squashed. Weight: it sinks and its shadow grows. Sharpness: the orb turns from round to a pointed star.
  • The real card draws this in 2D (CSS and canvas): no 3D engine, so the first visit does not grow. A 3D version (the gem already uses WebGL) is possible later; other shapes (a pebble, a cube) can be offered later.
  • Reduced motion: the change is instant.
  • Vibration (decided: yes): where the phone supports it, a switch (off until chosen) lets the person feel the surface. Pressure sets the strength, texture the pattern (smooth is one long soft pulse; rough is quick short ones), weight the length. It appears only if the device can vibrate, never carries meaning alone (the text line always says the same), and stops under reduced motion or when the person turns it off. Android browsers support it; iOS does not.

Notes

  • Today these are the five sliders inside “Add more detail” (ten in all, five for each state), recorded and described afterwards. Here they act on the surface.
  • Each slider can be left unset: choosing nothing is a choice (an unset state, as in the code).
  • Both states get this card (decided): a Now / Wish choice at the top switches which state the sliders shape, and each keeps its own settings.

Your landscape:

Where it sits

An optional activity inside Touch (owner, 2026-11-17), offered after "Feel it more" and before the last card. Skipping it changes nothing.

Tab order

  1. Emergency Exit
  2. Put this down
  3. Sky, far hills, near ground, water, path (five labelled lists)
  4. Surprise me, Clear, Skip, Next

A screen reader says

  • Five labelled lists, the line “Your landscape: sky silk, far hills wool, ...” as a polite status. The picture is described once as a landscape made from the choices; nothing depends on seeing it.

Responds live (try it)

  • Each list changes its part of the picture at once. Reduced motion: instant.
  • In the real card the surfaces are the existing texture pictures, laid into the shapes; here patterns stand in.
  • Optional: the temperature, weight and sharpness sliders from "Feel it more" can act on one layer at a time.

Open

Five layers (sky, far hills, near ground, water, path) are a draft. In the report the landscape is the text list, with a small picture if the person wants one.
