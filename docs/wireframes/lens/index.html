Lens wireframes (draft, 2026-11-17)

Plain HTML wireframes for the lens rebuild (docs/lens-rewrite-scope.md, docs/lens-rebuild-audit.md). They are built to be tested: keyboard, screen reader, reduced motion, and a text twin for anything heard. They send nothing and make no sound. Open the files in a browser from this folder.

  • The entry, the tray and the report: breathing first, six lenses to pick up and put down (not opened, looked at, in your report), the coach kept apart, and the report built from what was added.
  • Essence: the flavor of the life you have and the one you want: five taste dials draw a shape (now and wish together), notes and what lingers, a tasting note in words, add to my report.
  • The body map: one map for the whole report; paint it with the colors and surfaces you added; a list of places is the full alternative to the drawing.
  • Touch: how it feels now, how you would like it to feel (a surface each), feel it more (five sliders that change the surface), add to my report.
  • Sight: pick two colors (with a live strength slider), a palette, add to my report (three cards; the body map is separate).
  • Sound: make a melody, hear it your way (the sliders bend the melody), make it longer (when available), add to my report.

Every wireframe follows

  • Emergency Exit is first in the tab order.
  • One task per card; short copy (about grade 5 to 8).
  • Every slider has a live result and a text value, and an unset state.
  • Sound is off until asked and always has a text twin.
  • Nothing is timed; nothing scores, streaks or rewards returning.
