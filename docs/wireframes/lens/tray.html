The shape of a visit

Breathing is the default way in and is not a lens. From it a person can go on to reflect, which opens the tray of six lenses (picked up and put down in any order), or to the coach, a seventh lens held apart. The report is built from the lenses a person added.

Tab order

  1. Emergency Exit
  2. Settings
  3. (the breathing area is not a stop; the counter is read politely)
  4. Reflect with the lenses
  5. Talk with the coach

A screen reader says

  • The gem is described once; the counter says “in” and “out”. The two ways on are in a labelled navigation list.

What this replaces

The three-door triangle (breathe, reflect, coach). Breathing is the screen itself; the other two doors are the two quiet buttons. The glow and the three ring colors can stay as a visual accent.

The coach: a lens of its own

Kept apart from the six. A short conversation (five exchanges) with an AI. It is not therapy.

Tab order

  1. Emergency Exit
  2. Back to breathing
  3. Two skip links (the coach, my report)
  4. The six lenses, in a fixed order, each a button
  5. The body map button
  6. Make my report
  7. Talk with the coach

A screen reader says

  • A list of six items, each “Sight, not opened” (the state is in the same line, never only a color or an icon).
  • The coach is a separate region with its own heading, announced as kept apart.

Design points

  • Icons are Google's Material Symbols (outlined), bundled as SVG; each is decorative (alt="") because the name is always beside it, so nothing depends on seeing it.
  • The lens marks (to be drawn) sit beside the names; every lens is distinguishable without color.
  • The coach is visibly apart: a divider, a different shape, its own heading.
  • No “step 3 of 6”, no “complete all”, no required order.
