The Movement lens: five cards

All wireframes · Pattern · Draft 2026-11-17 · plan: docs/lens-audit-movement-pattern.md

How to read these

Movement and Pattern share one template. Each card is a real page of controls on the left (try the keyboard) and its notes on the right. Every card has Emergency Exit first in the tab order. Nothing here sends anything, makes a sound or saves.

Every lens opens with: choosing fewer can be more helpful than choosing many. Where you may choose several, three is a soft limit: a warning comes before a fourth, never a block. Try it on card 1.

Tab order

  4. The shapes, each a checkbox with its drawing named
  5. Skip, Next

A screen reader says

  • Each shape is a checkbox named by its label, then its one-sentence description of the drawing (never a feeling).
  • The count under the shapes is polite.
  • At a fourth choice the notice is announced once and focus moves to “Add it anyway”. “Keep three” returns focus to the shape.

Responds live

  • “One at a time” shows one shape with Previous and Next, for people who want fewer things on screen.

Not drawn here

The 24 shapes are the ones already in the site; eight are listed to show the pattern. Each keeps its drawing and its plain description.

  4. Speed, Energy
  5. More descriptions (closed by default), then what is inside
  6. Skip, Next

A screen reader says

  • The preview is a named image. The status line under it says the same thing in words every time a control changes: “upward, fast, very strong”. It never moves focus.
  • “More descriptions” is a button with an expanded or collapsed state.

Responds live (try it)

  • Speed sets how fast it moves. Energy sets how far and how bright. The direction words set which way. Under reduced motion it is a still with trails, and the words still change.

Tucked into More descriptions

Quality words, the free-text line, and the vibration opt-in (off until switched on, only where the device can, never the only signal). Skipping them never blocks adding the lens.

Added. You can come back and change it.

If put down without adding

Your report will say: “Examined, not included: Movement.” Anything you chose stays here, marked “not in your report yet”, until you leave. It is never saved unless you add it.

States

  • Before: as drawn.
  • Added: the card reopens with Update and Remove.
  • Examined: one report line, kept on the device only.

Playful, within the limits

A still “added” state and, if motion is allowed, one soft settle of the shape. No points, streaks or badges.
