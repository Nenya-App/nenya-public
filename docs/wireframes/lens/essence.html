The Essence lens: the flavor of your life

All wireframes · Tray · Touch · Draft 2026-11-17 · audit: docs/lens-audit-essence.md

The idea (owner, 2026-11-17)

Essence asks one question, twice: what is the flavor of the life you have, and the one you want? It is a flavor, not a meal: a person describes it the way a taster describes a drink, with five taste dials, a few notes, a feel in the mouth and what lingers. Nothing needs to be tasted or eaten; it works from imagination. A shape drawn from the dials shows now and wish together, and a tasting note says it in words.

A screen reader says

  • Each dial: “Sweet, none to very strong, strong.” The line “Right now: …” is the text twin and updates politely. The shape is described once and never needed: the words say the same.

Responds live (try it)

  • Moving a dial reshapes the five-pointed figure. It is a chart of the person’s answers, not a drawing of food. Reduced motion: it simply changes.

Notes

  • Each dial can stay at none; none is a choice.
  • The five basic tastes are the ones already in the code (senseWords.ts). Open: add “spicy heat” and “richness” as dials?
  • No food, diet or hunger language anywhere.

A screen reader says

  • Each dial as before; “From now to what you want: more sweet, less bitter” is a polite status, so the gap is heard as well as seen.

Responds live (try it)

  • Two shapes at once, now (dashed) and wish (solid), with a line on how they differ. A table of the ten values is available to a screen reader as the exact data.

Notes

Seeing the gap, with no judgement attached, is the point: nothing says one is better. “Start from my now” copies the first set so the person nudges only what differs.
