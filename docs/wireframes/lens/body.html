The body map: one map for the whole report

All wireframes · Tray · Touch · Draft 2026-11-17 · audit: docs/lens-audit-touch.md

What this is

Decided (owner, 2026-11-17): there is one body map for the whole report, not one per lens. It works the way the color picker does: pick a paint, then put it on a place. A paint is a color or a surface the person added in a lens (or any color from the picker). The map is reached from the tray and from each lens's last card, and it appears in the report as its own section.

A screen reader says

  • The list is the primary way, not a fallback: each place is a labelled select with the paint it carries. The drawing repeats it and is described as doing the same thing. “On the map now” is the text twin and updates politely.

Responds live (try it)

  • The brush (decided: yes, beside the places): turn on “Draw with a brush”, then drag on the picture; the marks are in the chosen paint and the size slider sets the width. The brush is for pointers and touch only; keyboard and screen-reader users have the places list, which is the full alternative. The text line counts the brush marks by paint (“12 dabs of steel blue”) so they are in words too. Undo removes the last stroke.
  • Pick a paint, then press a place on the drawing or choose from the list. The place fills with the color, or with the surface shown as its color in this wireframe (the real map shows the texture, applied the way the picker applies a color).

How it fits

  • Paints come from what the person added in Sight and Touch, plus any color from the picker. If no lens has been added yet, only the picker is offered.
  • The map belongs to the report, not to a lens: it shows in the report as its own section, with Update and Remove. Removing a lens does not remove the map; a mark that used a removed lens's paint keeps its color or texture.
  • Optional, and never needed for anything else.
