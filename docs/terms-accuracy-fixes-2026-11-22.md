# Terms accuracy fixes, 2026-11-22

Branch `claude/terms-accuracy-fixes`, cut from `origin/main` at `eaf69cf` (PR #34). This applies the owner's decisions on the factual audit of the Terms against the code (the audit itself, `REPORT.md` and `PROPOSED-WORDING.md`, is kept in the owner's private folder and is not in this repository).

**This is not legal advice, and nothing here says the Terms were reviewed by a lawyer or a clinician.** The owner's lawyer review is still pending. The aim is narrower: every sentence in the Terms, the Privacy text and the other public texts should say only what the code does today.

## 1. Terms sentences: old to new

Section numbers are those of `src/lib/legalDocument.ts`. "Switch" means the text depends on a build-time variable. The outgoing text is `docs/legal/archive/terms-and-privacy-until-2026-11-22.md` (rendered with `VITE_SCREEN_MODEL=on` and the page switches off, as the live site showed it).

### Section 15, "Transcripts" (audit finding 1; options 1A, 1B)
- Old: "...our server checks the signature, refuses an unsigned or altered transcript, and passes it on without keeping or logging it, through a separate form (Section 14)."
- New: you first see what will be sent, may add a note, and choose what we may do with it (test and improve the coach, never train a model; quote short parts without your name; show it on the public "witnessed conversations" page) and how long we keep it (30 days, 90 days, one year, or until you ask us to delete it). The server "keeps it in our own private storage until the time you chose; a person at Nenya reads it there". You can narrow or delete it with your signed file at nenya.biz/my-transcript. Keyed overlap codes are named and "deleted with the transcript". A short notice (fingerprint and choices, never the conversation) goes through the web host's form service. Public showing (1B): read and edited by a person first, comes down at any time, cannot be recalled once copied, names may be missed, never offered after a safety stop.
- Also: "We do not store the conversation; the signed file stays with you" became "We do not store the conversation unless you send it to us (below); ...". In "Where your words go", "...gone when you close it" gained "unless you choose to send a transcript to us (below)".
- Evidence: `netlify/functions/report.mts` (stores `inbox/<hash>`, `witnessed/` when publishing is allowed, the keyed overlap codes, the `verified-transcript` form alert; refuses publishing after a safety stop with `publish-not-allowed`); `src/lib/transcriptPolicy.ts` (`KEEPS` = 30d, 90d, 1y, until-asked; the three uses; `USE_LABELS`); `netlify/functions/transcript-expiry.mts` (`deleteEverything` removes the inbox, waiting and public copies and the overlap codes; runs `@daily`); `netlify/functions/transcript-revoke.mts`; `src/coach/TranscriptTools.tsx` (the choices screen has no switch).

### Section 19, "Retention" and "Your rights" (1C, 10, 3B)

### Section 14, The Color API and the word look-up (finding 3, option 3B)

### Section 15, "Where your words go" (finding 2, option 2A; finding 7, option 7A)
- Old (switch on only): "That is a separate request, made only when you press the button..." Old (switch off): nothing.
- New, both sets: "At the fifth exchange the coach's last reply may include a short summary. To write it, our server sends the conversation (your messages with identifiers replaced, and the coach's replies) to Venice AI once more, in the same way as a message. We do not store it. If any message was flagged by the checks, or your last message was critical of the coach or abusive toward it, no summary is written." I wrote "may include" rather than "includes" because the server only writes one when `COACH_FINAL_SUMMARY=call` and the checks pass. I added "or your last message was critical of the coach or abusive toward it" because `coach.mts` skips the summary when `flag` or `hostile` is set (`!flag && !hostile`); the audit's wording ("if any message was flagged") omitted the hostile case.
- Switch on only (the bracketed button sentence): "A summary you ask for with the button works the same way: the conversation goes to Venice AI once more, and Venice AI writes the summary. We do not store it."
- 7A: "...so that only the processing environment Venice describes as secure can read it... We check the proof Venice gives for that environment. For now, if one part of that check fails, we log it and carry on rather than stop the message." Evidence: `src/lib/veniceE2ee.ts` lines 150-157 (`hardwareGate`, default mode `report`: `console.warn` and carries on). The production value `COACH_HARDWARE_CHECK=report` is from the audit; I did not read production settings.
- Evidence for the summary: `coach.mts` around lines 1263-1295 and 1390-1400 (a second model call with the whole conversation so far, scrubbed, drawn at the same time as the closing, and appended with the site's transcript sentence).

### Section 15, "Check for automated traffic" (finding 4, option 4A)

## 6. Open questions for a lawyer
From the audit: **Q1** Minnesota law and Ramsey County venue; **Q2** the $100 cap and extending the disclaimer to Venice; **Q3** Sections 4 and 8 for a coach with a distress path; **Q4** GDPR bases, and whether a data processing agreement or transfer assessment is needed for Netlify, Venice or Cloudflare; **Q5** whether 30-day transcript hashes, one-way daily address codes and keyed overlap codes are personal data (this change names the overlap codes and the daily codes more openly); **Q6** a tick box for 18+, and the age 13 floor; **Q7** referral credits against "does not sell, share or monetize data"; **Q8** whether anonymized public transcripts are safe when names may be missed (the Terms now describe public showing; the choices screen and the page already existed); **Q9** `PrivacyPromise.tsx`: "absolute trust" and "making data extraction impossible" (**left unchanged on purpose, as instructed**); **Q10** whether Fractured Atlas must review the Terms, the referral arrangement or the transcript-sharing feature; **Q11** whether the effective-date mechanism is adequate when a change sits behind a switch (and, here, whether the corrected text may go live under the old date); **Q12** whether provider claims (no training, encrypted, no retention) may be in our own voice or should say "they say".

Added by this work: **Q13** the word "never" in "never to train a model" and "never shown after a safety stop": it describes the code and the choices screen, not a legal undertaking. **Q14** whether naming an enforcement change in advance ("we will say so here first", Turnstile and the model) is a promise the owner wants to be held to. **Q15** the hardware-check sentence: whether saying the check logs and carries on is better than switching `COACH_HARDWARE_CHECK` to `enforce` after a test (a code and settings decision, not a wording one). **Q16** whether "kept until we delete them" for the form notices is enough, given the notices hold fingerprints and choices; the audit suggested a promised time only if the owner will really delete on schedule.
