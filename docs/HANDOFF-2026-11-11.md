# Handoff: live gem, ghost-footprint stack, sealed coach, partner practice (2026-11-11)

Start here. This file lists everything built in the 2026-11-11 sessions, where it lives, what is switched on, and what is
left, so a later session can pick it up. Everything is in this repository on the `dev` branch. Nothing new is visible to
visitors until a switch is turned on.

## Read first
1. `docs/ghost-footprint-stack.md`: the whole plan, component by component, with the footprint numbers.
2. `docs/live-gem.md`: the ray-traced gem and the `/bench/` tuning page.
3. `docs/drafts/terms-sealed-and-partner-draft.md`: the Terms & Privacy draft (not applied).

## Switches (all off by default)

| What | How to turn it on | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| Live gem for the whole site | `GEM_ENGINE = 'live'` in `src/lib/gemSprite.ts` | Drops the 809 KB sprite download where WebGL2 works |
| Sealed coach | Env `COACH_SEALED=on` (Functions scope; needs an `e2ee-` model, which is the default) | `/coach` shows a Witnessed/Sealed choice after the colours |
| Partner practice | Env `CELL_ENABLED=true` | `/cell` opens; the About page's Partner Practice section links to it |

Try them on a deploy preview first. Apply the Terms draft before switching either on in production.

## Pages
- `/bench/`: the sprite gem and the live gem side by side, with panels for the inner and outer orbits, the gem's light,
  cut and quality, and the device tier. Not linked, not indexed.
- `/coach`: unchanged unless `COACH_SEALED=on`. It then shows the mode choice (`src/coach/ModeChooser.tsx`), and sealed
  conversations use `src/coach/SealedChat.tsx`.
- `/cell`: partner practice (`src/cell/CellPage.tsx`).
  - Make or join a cell, wait for the other person, compare the safety colours, then talk.
  - Either person can pause. A crisis message pauses the cell automatically and shows resources.
  - The reader can report any message from the other person, and either person can close and delete the cell.
  - Not indexed while being tested.

## Not verified here

   Venice accepts a client key made in the browser, and that its streamed pieces open in the browser.
