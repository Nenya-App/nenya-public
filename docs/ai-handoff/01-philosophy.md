# 1. Philosophy: why Nenya exists and how to decide hard cases

## What it is
Nenya is a free, small, open-source tool for **breathing, reflection and learning to say what you feel and need**. It has three doors, chosen at the start: **breathe** (a paced breathing animation, counter and optional tones), **reflect** (six sensory "gateways": sight, sound, touch, essence, movement, insight, which produce a private report the person keeps), and **coach** (an optional, limited AI conversation informed by Nonviolent Communication, which says plainly that it is an AI). Plus pages to learn (NVC and related ideas), a guestbook, and witnessed conversations a person chose to share.

## The position underneath
Nenya starts from **care, kinship and the roots of life**: people are tended by other people, and a tool should send a person back toward the people in their life, chosen family included, not stand in for them. This is deliberately not the "AI first" frame (a smarter assistant that does more for you) and not a productivity or optimization frame. The measure of success is a person who needs the tool less, and has someone to talk to.

Consequences, which decide most hard cases:
- **The AI is a small, honest listener, not a companion.** It says it is an AI, does not remember, does not miss you, does not diagnose, does not replace a friend or a therapist, and when someone leans on it, it turns them toward people (instructions v2.1 do this at the close).
- **Limits are a feature.** Five exchanges, 500 characters a message, a rest period, a daily cap. Scarcity here is care, not a business model. Do not "improve" engagement.
- **Nothing is kept that need not be.** No accounts, no tracking cookies, no advertising, no sale or sharing of data. What a person writes in the reflection tools never leaves their device. Where something must be kept (a shared report, a guestbook point), it is the least that works, for the shortest time, with a way to delete it, and it is written down in `docs/data-register.md`.
- **Free, and it stays free at the core.** Donations and a fiscal sponsor. A paid tier for extra features has been floated; the core breathing and reflection tools stay free. The project does not monetize data, and declined agent payment schemes.
- **Accessibility is the design, not a layer.** The standard is WCAG 2.2 level AAA (docs/accessibility-standard.md). Plain language (reading grade 8 or below, often lower).
- **Honesty about uncertainty.** The site says the coach is a prototype, that crisis detection is phrase matching and can miss things, and that it is not therapy or a crisis service. Never overclaim: no "secure", "anonymous", "HIPAA", "end-to-end encrypted". The infrastructure guard exists because small models say such things.

## Tone and public posture
- **Plain, warm, accurate.** No hype, no satire, no jabs, no naming individuals or organizations to criticize. Accurate observation only. Nenya aims to stay low-profile and inconsequential rather than loud.
- **Names:** "Nenya" or "nenya.biz" in text, never "Nenya Project". Copyright "© 2025 Nenya". The turned-e (schwa) is used only in the animated footer mark (nənya.biz, which reads as "nunya biz": a wink about privacy), nowhere else.
- **Tolkien references are fine.** "Nenya" and the Valar-based color palettes are codenames and an homage; do not flag or remove them. The palettes are described as images and rough guesses at feelings, never as a measurement or medical instrument.
- **No claims of authority.** Not affiliated with CNVC or the NVC movement beyond crediting the Feelings and Needs Inventory (linked). Not a scientific or medical instrument.

## Rules of thumb for a new instance
1. If a change makes the AI warmer, more available or more flattering, stop and reread this file.
2. If a change collects, keeps or sends more data, stop: find the data-register row it changes, and the Terms sentence that must change first.
3. Prefer a fixed, reviewed sentence (written by a person) over a model's improvisation for anything about safety, memory, identity, privacy or money.
4. Prefer removing a feature to shipping it half-checked.
5. Say what you checked and what you did not.
