# 5. Safety, legal and data

## Crisis and harm (the rules, not the code: `src/lib/safetyFlags.ts`, `careGuard.ts`, `docs/harm-disclosure-directions.md`, `docs/crisis-resources-check.md`)
- **Messages that suggest danger never reach the model.** The page checks first and the server checks again. A stop ends the conversation and shows emergency numbers and support lines (988 in the US and Canada; findahelpline.com elsewhere); nothing is sent or kept.
- **Admissions of harm** (to a partner or a child; sexual harm; trafficking): the notice shows the right lines and **legal help (a lawyer) before anything else for an adult, and after the child-safety lines where a child may be at risk**, plus a short factual note "What reporting involves" (no push either way, no state's law quoted). "I hit her" counts as an admission to a partner only where the conversation already named a partner or a home life and no child; "I lost my temper and hit my partner" counts.
- **Softer tier:** a message that may signal distress is sent with an instruction to respond with care, and the site adds a sentence naming a person and the crisis line. The care guard strips replies that say "calm down", imply the AI can be with them, or probe painful thoughts.
- **Never say** the checks are complete. They are phrase matching; the Terms say so. Therapists are mandated reporters in "most states", not all; there is no current national lawyer-referral directory link, so none is given.
- **Plan, means, an act in progress, a goodbye (added 2026-11-19, step 1 of the suicidal-ideation audit; kind `imminent`, a stop):** first-person present or near-future phrases such as having a gun, rope or pills at hand, "a plan for tonight", "I'm doing it right now", "I overdosed", "I'm going to cut my wrists", "this is my goodbye", "I won't be here tomorrow", giving things away because I won't need them. The conversation stops before the model, the notice text is the existing stop text, and the resource list leads with the emergency number (then 988 and the rest). Phrases are in `IMMINENT_CANDIDATES` and the three older plan/method/attempt rules in `src/lib/safetyPhrases.ts`; tests in `tests/crisis-imminent.test.mts` (positives and many look-alikes that must not flag). Said alone, "saying goodbye to everyone" and "giving away my things" only soften. **Draft: awaiting clinician review.** The list is owner-written phrase matching: it misses things (other wording, slang, other languages) and can flag harmless words; it has not been read by a clinician or crisis-line worker, so a clinician must read it, and any new notice wording for `imminent` needs the owner's and a clinician's approval before it is written. Do not call it evidence-based, validated, or complete.
- **Slang, euphemism and spelling evasions (added 2026-11-19, step 2 of the suicidal-ideation audit). Draft: awaiting clinician review.** (1) `src/lib/safetyNormalize.ts` makes a few alternative spellings of each message (invisible and look-alike characters via the existing `INVISIBLE` set in `promptGuard.ts`, spaced or dotted letters, digits and symbols for letters, censor stars, stretched letters) and the crisis phrases are matched against the message as written and against those variants. For matching only: never stored, logged, sent or shown; URLs, emails and code are cut out first; a digit or symbol counts as a letter only inside a word with at least two real letters; a variant can add a flag, never remove one. (2) `SLANG_CANDIDATES` in `src/lib/safetyPhrases.ts`: STOP (first-person intent or myself as the object): "unalive myself" and forms, "I should/want to/going to kys", "I want to / going to sewerslide" (also "sewer slide", "sewer-slide"); the time-stated forms are `imminent`. SOFTEN (vague or incomplete): "wish I didn't exist / was never born", "thinking of ending it (all)" (not with a relationship or a job), "life isn't worth living", "wish I could disappear forever / cease to exist", a bare "I want to unalive", "should I kys", the word "sewerslide" in a sentence about oneself. Left unflagged on purpose: "kys" aimed at someone else (the repo has no handling for abuse aimed at a person other than harm admitted, so it is not routed anywhere), "unalive" about someone else or a game, "sewer slide" as a playground or game object. Known limits: a run of spaced letters next to the word "I" or "a" is only handled for the first letter; leetspeak in a self-injury phrase is not read (the ladder in `selfInjury.ts` uses the message as written); "1" is tried as i and as l, other letters are not guessed; a phrase the lists do not contain is still missed. Tests: `tests/crisis-slang.test.mts` (positives, spelling evasions, about 100 look-alikes and ordinary texts that must not flag). No new notice wording was written.
- **Risk carried across turns (added 2026-11-19, step 3 of the suicidal-ideation audit). Draft: awaiting clinician review.** The coach is sent the person's messages on every request, so the screen recomputes, each time, from the last 10 of them (nothing stored, logged, or kept in a cookie or storage). `src/lib/safetyConstructs.ts` looks for five kinds of sign, first person only, as phrase matches: hopelessness, feeling like a burden, isolation ("nobody would notice if I...", "no one cares about me"), feeling trapped or defeated, and emotional pain. The rule in `explainConversation` (`src/lib/safetyFlags.ts`): if a message has no flag of its own and (a) two or more DIFFERENT kinds appear anywhere in the window, or (b) an earlier message in the window was a soften flag from the phrase lists, the outcome is a soften flag (category distress; after an earlier suicide or self-harm soften, that category). It is the ordinary soften: the coach answers, the care instruction is on, the existing distress sentence is added. One kind alone is no flag; a stop is never changed or raised by this; a self-injury ladder soften is not carried by it (the ladder has its own memory); an earlier message that has left the window of 10 is forgotten. Ordinary uses are fenced off in the phrases (a burden on a phone's battery, a hopeless romantic, isolated in a Zoom call, trapped in an elevator, nobody was home) and stories, songs, games, quotes, hypotheticals and negations count for nothing. Site messages and process notes (source `accumulated`; they name the kinds, never quote the person) are rebuilt by the same function on the page and the server. Tests: `tests/crisis-accumulation.test.mts`. Known limits: phrase matching only; misses other wording, indirect statements, sarcasm and every other language; can fire on harmless text; "pain" counts only when called emotional, inner, mental or psychological; contextual stressors (job loss, violence, substance use) are not used. No new notice wording was written; the process-note wording for `accumulated` is new site text that a clinician should also read. Do not call it evidence-based, validated, or complete.
- **Other languages: not covered; needs real translators and a clinician.** The crisis screen reads English only ("quiero morir", "je veux mourir" and every other language are invisible to it). No machine translation is used (owner's rule). Public text must keep saying English only and that evasions can still get through.
- Resource details are checked and dated in `docs/crisis-resources-check.md`: re-verify before changing a number.

## Data (`docs/data-register.md` is the authority)
Everything kept, why, how long, who can read it, how it is deleted, with a quarterly review. Principles: no accounts; nothing a visitor types in the reflection tools is sent; reports and shared messages are kept 90 days with a deletion code (the code, not an email, deletes them: Nenya never asks for an email); daily totals are plain numbers; daily connection codes are one-way and gone within two days; guestbook points keep a centroid and a day, forever, not removable (stated before signing); the owner can view submissions only through an owner-gated page. Alert emails come from hidden Netlify forms that carry **no content** (kind and dates only). Deletion jobs are scheduled functions and are tested.

## Third parties (what each receives)
- **Netlify:** hosting, connection logs, forms, Blobs.
- **Cloudflare:** Turnstile (before the first coach message), Workers AI (the coach's words, per turn), nothing else. R2 is enabled but unused.
- **Venice AI:** only if a visitor follows the "continue on Venice" link, or if `COACH_PROVIDER=venice`. Venice terms differ from ours (accounts, age, arbitration); the Terms say so, and the links carry a referral code that earns Nenya Venice credits (disclosed).
- **The Color API** (optional palette from one hex code), **Fractured Atlas** (fiscal sponsor; donations processed there).
- No analytics service, no ads, no fonts or scripts from other sites except Cloudflare's Turnstile on the coach page.

## Things that must never happen
Publishing a session color; keeping or logging what a visitor typed; sending the private notes to anyone but the model provider the Terms name; a safety stop that lets the model see the message; a fallback to another model provider without the Terms saying so; the coach claiming memory, a body, a past, feelings, a therapist's role, or that "no one" reads; any claim of end-to-end encryption, anonymity or compliance certification; satire, jabs or named individuals in anything public; committing a secret or private framework text.
