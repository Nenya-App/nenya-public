# Contributing to Nenya as a professional or an NVC practitioner

Status: current as of 2026-11-25. A one-page invitation in plain words.

## Where Nenya stands
Nenya is a free, early prototype: a breathing tool, a reflection tool, and an optional AI coach. It has no accounts and no tracking. No study has been done on it, and no clinician has reviewed it. We are asking for help because we want to get the words right. We are not asking you to put your name behind a finished product.

## Who we are looking for
- Counselors, therapists, social workers, and crisis-line workers.
- Trauma-informed and domestic-abuse advocates.
- Certified or experienced NVC trainers and practitioners.
- Teachers and writers who know plain-language and reading-level work.
- Anyone with lived experience who wants to read and react.

## What a contribution could look like
1. **Read and mark up safety wording.** The messages shown in a hard moment. Tell us what is unclear, unkind, or risky.
2. **Check the crisis resources.** Are the lines current and right for your country or community? Suggest better ones.
3. **Review the coach's NVC.** Does the coach's way of reflecting feelings and needs sound like NVC, or like a script? Where does it go wrong?
4. **Review how the coach answers contempt.** What it says when someone talks about a group as less than human, and when it offers a learning card.
5. **Check the learning cards.** Sources, accuracy, tone, reading level.
6. **Write or edit a short piece.** A practice, an explanation, a worked example. New pieces are written fresh, with credit.
7. **Name what is missing.** Situations, groups, or phrases we have not thought of.

Small is fine. One page of notes is a real contribution. What a reviewer reads is set out in [reviewers-guide.md](reviewers-guide.md).

## What we ask of you
- Say what you looked at and what you would change.
- Do not paste in text you do not have the right to share. This matters for NVC in particular: the feelings and needs lists follow the CNVC inventory, which belongs to CNVC. We credit and link to them, and we do not relicense their wording. Write your own words, and name any source.
- Do not send us anyone's private details, including clients'.
- Tell us your limits. "I am not a clinician" or "I only know the US" helps us as much as an expert opinion.

## What you can expect from us
- **Credit** the way you choose, or no credit at all.
- **Honesty about what a review means.** We will say a person read it. We will not say it is clinically approved, and we will not use your name or title to suggest more than you did.
- **Your say on your name.** If we want to quote you, we ask first.
- **Contact details** kept only to talk with you, never shared.
- **No pay** for now. Nenya is free and has no income. We would rather say so plainly than hint at it.

## About the terms
Everything given to the project would be shared under the project's open license (AGPL-3.0 or later). You keep your own copyright. The name "Nenya" and the logo are not part of that license. **No contributor agreement is in force yet.** Until that changes, we will not promise terms we do not have. Write to us first, and we will tell you where things stand.

## How to start
Write to **info@nenya.biz**. Say what you do and what you would like to look at. Code changes go to the `dev` branch, but you do not need to touch code. We can send the exact wording in a document.
