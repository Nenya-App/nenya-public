## The sixteen palettes

Revised 2026-11-09. The palettes are simple starting points, not a measurement: nothing here scores or decodes a color, and no color has a fixed meaning. Each is chosen by hand around the image its archetype stands for. The first color is the palette's main color. The same six colors are used by the breathing logo, the gem, the Pantheon page and the color picker (`src/app/components/ValarBreathingLogo.tsx` is the source). Checked 2026-11-09 for how easy the colors are to tell apart (including red-green and blue-green color blindness); most pairs differ clearly, a few are weaker (Ember, Greenwood, Soft Rain).
