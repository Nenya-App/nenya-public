## What you will make

You will make your own copy of this site on your own computer. It has the breathing tool, the six Lenses, the sound, and the report you can save as a file. You can change how it looks and what it says. Only you can open it.

You will not need to be a developer. You will copy some commands and paste them in.

## What is left out

The AI coach is not part of this. It needs an outside AI service and a server, so it cannot run privately on your computer.

Forms that send a message or a report will not work in your copy, for the same reason.

## What you need

- A computer. These steps are written for a Mac first, then Windows and Linux. Only the Mac steps have been tried.
- An internet connection once, to get the code and its parts.
- A free program called Node.js, version 24 or newer. Get it from [nodejs.org](https://nodejs.org).
- About an hour.
- Room on your disk for about 500 megabytes.

The version of Node.js that the project asks for is written in [`package.json`](https://github.com/Nenya-App/nenya-public/blob/main/package.json).
