## A copy just for you

You may read, run and change the code on your own computer.

## If others will use it

This guide is a plain summary. It is not legal advice. Read the license itself, and ask a lawyer if it matters.

The code is under the AGPL-3.0 license. In short:

- If you give your changed copy to other people, they must get it under the same license, with the source code.
- If you run a changed copy on a server and people use it over a network, you must offer them its source code.
- Keep the license file and the notices about who wrote the code.
- There is no warranty.

The full text is in [`LICENSE`](https://github.com/Nenya-App/nenya-public/blob/main/LICENSE). The people who wrote the license explain it at [gnu.org](https://www.gnu.org/licenses/agpl-3.0.html).

## The name and the logo

The license does not give you the name Nenya, the way it is written, or the logo. Do not use them for your copy, and do not suggest that nenya.biz made or approves it. The rules are in [`TRADEMARK.md`](https://github.com/Nenya-App/nenya-public/blob/main/TRADEMARK.md).

## Before you share, check

1. You changed the name, logo, icons and contact address, as in the fifth guide.
2. You replaced the Terms and Privacy Policy with your own, and they are true for your copy.
3. Your source code is available to the people who use it.
4. You say plainly what your copy is. If it touches how people feel, say that it is not therapy and not a crisis service.
5. You tested what it sends, as in the sixth guide. A copy on the internet behaves differently from one on your computer.
