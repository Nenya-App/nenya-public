# DRAFT: Terms and Privacy Promise wording for sending a conversation (not applied)

Written 2026-11-10 for the owner and a lawyer (Fractured Atlas may review). It replaces the transcript parts of `docs/transcript-consent-scope.md` where they differ. Nothing here is live. When applied, archive the current Terms, bump the effective date, and update the About answers that point at them (`n-transcripts` and the coach answers). **Decided by the owner:** no training of any kind is offered; sending a conversation is a **separate consent** from the site's general terms (the site stays browsable by anyone); sending needs "I am 18 or older, and these are my own words"; and the Terms may **recommend that anyone under 18 use Nenya with a trusted adult with them or looking on.**

## 1. A new Terms section: "Sending a conversation to Nenya"
> **This is separate from using the site.** Anyone can use Nenya's tools and the coach without an account. Sending a conversation to us is a different, optional step, and it has its own agreement, which you give each time you do it.
>
> **Who may send one.** You must be 18 or older, and the words must be your own. Before anything is sent, you confirm: "I am 18 or older, and these are my own words." If you are younger, you are welcome to use Nenya, and we recommend using it with a trusted adult with you or looking on, but please do not send a conversation. We rely on your statement; we do not check identity, and a person who reads a conversation before it is shown also looks for signs that the writer is under 18 and, if there are any, does not show or keep it.
>
> **What you may choose.** Reading it: we always read what you send, because that is why you sent it. Beyond that, you may choose (1) to let us keep it, with names and contact details removed, among the conversations we use to check and improve the coach and its safety checks; (2) to let us quote short parts, never with your name; and (3) to let us show it on the Witnessed Conversations page, without your name, after the site removes names and contact details again and a person reads and edits it by hand, and may decide not to show it. You choose how long we keep it and, if you choose to have it shown, which parts and for how long. **None of this is chosen for you.**
>
> **What we do not do.** We do not use any conversation to train or tune a model, and we do not offer that choice. We do not sell it, use it for advertising, or publish it as a dataset. We do not combine it with anything that identifies you.
>
> **Your words and the AI's.** You keep ownership of your own words. The AI's replies are written by a machine; we treat them as part of the conversation, not as yours. You give us permission for the uses you chose, for as long as you chose, and no more. You promise the words are yours and that you have not included anyone else's private details. We replace names and contact details automatically and a person checks, but neither is perfect.
>
> **Your consent receipt.** After you send, we can give you a **signed consent receipt**: a small file that says which conversation (by its fingerprint), what you agreed to, for how long, that you confirmed you are 18 or older, which version of this wording applied, and when. It contains no part of the conversation. Keep it with your signed transcript.
>
> **Taking it back.** With your signed transcript file you can, at nenya.biz/my-transcript, stop any use, take a public copy down, or ask for it to be deleted. We stop and delete what we keep. A shown copy comes down at once. **What cannot be undone:** if someone saved or copied a public copy while it was shown, we cannot get it back, and we do not promise otherwise.
>
> **How long we keep it.** For the time you chose, or until you ask us to delete it, and we delete it by itself when the time ends.
>
> **We may decline.** We may decide not to keep, use or show a conversation, for example if it cannot be made unidentifiable. A conversation in which our safety check stopped a message is never offered for the public page.
