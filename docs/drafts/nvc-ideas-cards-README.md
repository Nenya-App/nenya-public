**Honest note on the source.** I wrote these from the NVC tradition (Rosenberg's book is the one cited and linked) in our own words. I did **not** go through the coach training material to write them, and **no transcript text is used**. The trainer's way of teaching each idea, and her own examples, are what her review (and yours) should add. Each card says so in its `unsure` list.

**Credit and review.** Each card carries a `reviewers` note: you (pending) and the trainer ("requested: to add to, strike or refine"). The public credit to her is not in any card yet; the wording and the place (a card's source line, the About page, or both) are yours to decide. A card can carry a second source entry once her review is in.

**Not wired yet.** The cards are not offered by anything: `OFFER_MAP` (`src/lib/learningOffer.ts`) maps only language found by the lexicon. Offers for these ten come with the Learn stance (when a message shows the signs listed in each card's `shown-when`, only in Learn, one per exchange, at most two per conversation, never in distress or on the last exchange).
