# Plain-language summaries for the About pages: DRAFTS for you to edit

Written 2026-11-14. **Nothing here is on the site.** WCAG 3.1.5 (AAA) asks for text at about a lower-secondary reading level (roughly grade 9) **or a plain-language summary** of the text that is above it. These are the summaries, one per page whose text tests above that level. Each is written to be about grade 8 or lower (estimated; the grade after the arrow). They only restate what the page already says; where a page hedges ("projections", "a goal, not a feature") the summary keeps the hedge.

**How they would appear:** a short box with the heading "In plain words" at the top of each page, above the full text, which stays as it is. A person who finds the page hard can read the box and stop. (If you prefer them elsewhere, or not on a page, say so.)

**How to edit:** change anything. Every sentence is yours to cut or rewrite. Please check each one against your own meaning, not only against the page: I summarized from the page text as written, and I have not checked any of the research claims the pages make. Send me the edited file or tell me which to apply, and I will put them in with a test that each page has its box and that the box stays short (the page text is not changed).

## About the Breathing Tool
*Now: grade 15.6. Summary: grade 4.7.*

> **In plain words.** The pulsing logo on the welcome screen is there to help you breathe, not just to look nice. It moves at about six breaths a minute. Breathing at that pace is linked in research to a calmer body, and to a steadier heartbeat and blood pressure. To use it, breathe in as the logo brightens and out as it softens. Think of it as a first calm moment before you reflect. It is one way to settle, not a medical treatment.

## Evolved Leadership
*Now: grade 15.3. Summary: grade 7.7.*

> **In plain words.** This page says that leadership is not only for people with titles. It is something everyone can practice, moment to moment, and it grows through caring for each other. It points to research on emotions, emotional intelligence and compassion as support for that idea. It also argues that ignoring this has a large cost, in work, health and society, and cites reports on those costs. Nenya is offered as a small, private place to practice noticing your feelings and needs with kindness. The page lists its sources and asks you to check the claims yourself.

## Our Bio-Social Design
*Now: grade 15.3. Summary: grade 7.3.*

> **In plain words.** People are built to feel better through close connection with others and with the living world. This page argues that modern life often cuts us off from that, and that some of us then try to soothe ourselves by buying and consuming things. It says this habit adds to harm to the planet. Nenya is designed to help you notice what you feel and what you need, so that you rely less on that cycle. The page also explains design choices: the breathing logo, the six gateways, gentle questions, and showing deep ideas only when you want them. Some of its claims are projections, not measured results, and the page says so.

## Human-Centered Design
*Now: grade 14.2. Summary: grade 5.8.*

> **In plain words.** Human-centered design starts with the people who will use something. Nenya was shaped this way. People notice their inner world in different ways, so there are six gateways: color, sound, touch, smell and taste, movement and thought. The deep ideas stay out of the way until you ask for them. Privacy and anonymity are built in from the start. The page also links to more about this way of designing.

## The Pantheon of Needs
*Now: grade 13.8. Summary: grade 4.5.*

> **In plain words.** The Pantheon is a set of sixteen symbolic figures. Each stands for a need that matters to you as a person and to the group you live in. Each has a palette of six colors, with one main color first. The palettes are starting points so you do not have to begin from the whole range of color. They are not a test and measure nothing. The breathing animation moves slowly through all of them.

## Our NVC+ Method
*Now: grade 13.5. Summary: grade 6.3.*

> **In plain words.** Nonviolent Communication (NVC) is a way of talking with compassion, made by Dr. Marshall Rosenberg. It has four steps: say what you observe, name what you feel, name the need behind the feeling, and make a clear request that is an invitation, not a demand. NVC+ adds two ideas. Our feelings and needs are shaped by power and history, so frustration is not always only personal. And there are needs we share with others, such as fairness and care for the planet. Nenya is a private place to practice. It does not replace learning NVC from people. The page lists trusted places to learn and welcomes criticism of NVC or of using AI for this.

## Our Structure
*Now: grade 13.3. Summary: grade 7.2.*

> **In plain words.** Nenya is an independent art and technology project, not a startup. It takes no investors and gives no funder a seat on a board or a say in decisions. It is sponsored by Fractured Atlas, a nonprofit that handles donations. Donations are tax-deductible as the law allows. Nenya's code is public, so anyone can check what the site does. The aim is a tool that makes nothing from your data: no tracking, no advertising, nothing sold.

## Theory U
*Now: grade 13.1. Summary: grade 3.7.*

> **In plain words.** Theory U is an idea from Otto Scharmer. Change begins when you slow down and notice what is really there, then act from what you find. It goes down, then up, like the letter U. In Nenya, the gateways help you notice how you feel now. The NVC practice helps you name your real feelings and needs. From there you can choose a request or an action that fits what you need. Nenya is a daily place to practice this inner work.

## About the Designer
*Now: grade 12.5. Summary: grade 7.3.*

> **In plain words.** Nenya is made by Estëlle, a designer, teacher and artist in Minnesota. This page is here so you know who is behind the site. She asks what it would mean to build technology that helps people feel more human. Her work is guided by compassion, including for grief: seeing suffering clearly instead of looking away. The ideas behind Nenya are the sixteen needs, and naming needs without blame. Nenya is a practice of seeing yourself clearly and being patient with yourself and others.

## Our Privacy Promise
*Now: grade 12.1. Summary: grade 4.6.*

> **In plain words.** Nenya is built so that it has little to learn about you. There are no accounts or profiles. What you enter in the reflection tools stays on your device and is gone when you close the page. There is no tracking and no advertising. The optional coach sends your message through our server, which removes some personal details, to an AI model. We do not keep it. The AI can be wrong and has biases, and it gives no medical, legal or therapy advice. The page also lists privacy features we hope to build. None of them exist yet.

## Support Us
*Now: grade 11.2. Summary: grade 6.0.*

> **In plain words.** Nenya is a free place to breathe and reflect, with no advertising and no profiles. The plan is to keep the core tools free. Donations go through Fractured Atlas, the nonprofit that sponsors Nenya, and are tax-deductible as the law allows. The money pays for servers and the ongoing work. No funder gets a board seat or a say in decisions.

## The Six Gateways
*Now: grade 11.2. Summary: grade 4.7.*

> **In plain words.** People feel their inner world in different ways. Some feel it as color, some as sound, touch, smell and taste, movement or thought. Nenya has a gateway for each. The gateways help you find your own words. They do not tell you what your feelings mean. Today each gateway works as a reflection on your own. What you enter stays in your browser. The gateways are also about access: if one does not work for you, for example if you cannot see color, another can.

## The Power of Color
*Now: grade 11.2. Summary: grade 5.8.*

> **In plain words.** Color is an old human language, and much of the brain works on what we see. Research links colors with mood and attention, but only a little, and differently for different people and cultures. In Nenya you choose two colors: one for how you feel now and one for how you wish to feel. They are a short, private way to say something without words. Your colors are just codes. They are not an account and nothing identifies you. If color does not work for you, the page suggests other pictures, such as a sound, a weather scene or a texture.

## Terms of Use and Privacy Policy
*Now: grade 10.1. Summary: grade 4.9.*

> **In plain words.** This is a plain summary, not the legal text. If anything differs, the full Terms control. Nenya is free to use, with no accounts and no ads. What you enter in the reflection tools stays on your device. The optional coach sends your message through our server, which removes some personal details, to an AI model.
