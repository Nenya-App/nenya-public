# Image candidates from the Met and Openverse (scan of 2026-11-09)

Status: a **list to review**, not a selection. Made by searching the Met Open Access API (`/v1.1/search`, public-domain objects with images only) and the Openverse API (`license=cc0,pdm`, `mature=false`) for words that fit the Touch, Movement, Insight (pattern) and Essence gateways. Only text records were fetched; no image was downloaded and **I have not looked at any image**.

What this list is and is not:
- Results are in each service's own relevance order, the first few per search. A good search word does not make a good picture: every pick still needs eyes on it.
- "Possible people" is a flag from words in the title, classification and tags (portrait, figure, dancer, god...). It misses some and flags some wrongly. Those rows are left out of the Met list; look again if a search seems thin. Images with identifiable people are not wanted.
- Licenses: the Met marks these objects public domain, and says its open-access images and data are CC0; Openverse reports `cc0` or `pdm` (public domain mark). Both claims are **to be checked on each object's own page** before use (not verified here). `pdm` is a statement that the work is out of copyright, not a waiver: the credit is still given.
- Openverse is mostly Flickr (about 60% of the results), stock sites and Wikimedia. For Flickr and stock-site items the license is the uploader's claim, so provenance is weaker; only museum-like sources are listed below, and the other counts are noted per search.
- Only the first 18 results of each Met search (and the first 20 of each Openverse search) were examined, so "shown" counts are small after filtering. Some multi-word searches matched nothing at the Met (for example "basket weaving"); they should be rerun with single words. Openverse's museum-like sources are thin for most words: most of its CC0/PDM images are from Flickr and stock sites, so **the Met is the stronger source** and Wikimedia Commons next.
- No alt text has been written. That is the owner's review, after the pictures are chosen.
- Searches were run with a slow pace and no key. Terms and rate limits of both services are not verified.

Shortlisting rule of thumb: for **Touch**, close-up surfaces with a clear texture; for **Essence**, plants, fruit, spices and vessels without people; for **Movement** and **Insight**, the SVG route comes first (see the scope), and these are for reference.
