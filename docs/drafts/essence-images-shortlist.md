# Essence images: first shortlist (kept 2026-11-09)

Status: a draft for the owner. These are the 24 food photographs kept in the review page (from StockSnap and Rawpixel, found through Openverse) for the **taste and scent** fields, for now. Everything below is a suggestion to edit.

What was checked, and what was not:
- Each record was read from the Openverse API on 2026-11-09 and every one reports license **CC0**. The licence must still be read on each original page (links below) before use: **not verified here**.
- I looked at a small preview of each image (a contact sheet), not the full-size file. No person, hand or face is visible in any of the 24. Branded packaging: only the candy in row 13.
- The alt text is a first draft from the small previews. The owner reviews alt text; every line should be checked against the full picture.
- The word links are suggestions from the word lists in `src/lib/senseWords.ts`, never a fixed meaning: the picture is something to point at, and a person's own choice of words stays theirs.

## Notes
- Credits: CC0 needs no credit, but the credits page should list creator, source and link for each image anyway, and the letter to the source (StockSnap or Rawpixel) can invite them to see their photographs used. Rawpixel gives no creator for several of these.
- Numbers 11, 13, 15, 16 and 19 have a flag above: a title that does not match the picture, a brand, an unclear subject, a poisonous mushroom, and an insect.
- Mix: the 24 lean toward sweet and baked things and herbs. There is nothing for sour, salty, umami or fermented, and little for smoke, sea, earth or animal scents. A second search can fill these (for example olives, pickles, soy, salt, miso, smoked foods, seaweed, wet soil).
- Where the files would live: bundled with the site (never hotlinked), resized to a few tens of KB, with the credit and alt text in a data file (see `docs/gateway-images-scope.md`).
